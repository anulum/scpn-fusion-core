# Neuromorphic vertical stability control

Status: dated preprint prepared on 13 November 2026, describing commit
`8df066b60bf7` (4 September 2026, between releases 4.0.0 and 4.1.0); the
reduced-order benchmark was rerun on the 4.1.0 line.  Not deposited or submitted.

This manuscript evaluates the implemented PID, LQR and push-pull spiking
controllers on one declared reduced-order vertical-instability model. It
retains the spiking controller's failure, preserves the exact legacy latency
output without treating it as a ranking, documents the schema-v3 wiring
assessment, and pre-registers a blocked fresh accuracy-equivalent cohort.

- Review PDF: `manuscript.pdf`

- Reproducible figures: `figures/`
- Raw and generated evidence: `evidence/`

No fresh promotion cohort, hardware timing, power, irradiation,
plasma-discharge or production-control claim is made.
